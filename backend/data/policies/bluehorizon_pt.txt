PAYER: BLUEHORIZON INSURANCE
TITLE: BH-REHAB-02 Outpatient Physical Therapy (CPT 97110, 97140)

BlueHorizon Insurance covers outpatient physical therapy when there is a clinical expectation of measurable functional improvement. Payment may be adjusted when documentation does not support the number or frequency of units billed (CARC 151).

Coverage criteria for therapy beyond 20 visits per calendar year:
1. Documentation must show measurable functional progress using objective measures such as range of motion, strength testing, WOMAC, DASH, or Oswestry scores.
2. A plan of care with measurable goals must be documented and reviewed at least every 30 days.
3. Therapeutic exercise (CPT 97110) is billed in 15-minute timed units, and each unit must be supported by documented treatment time; up to 4 units per visit are covered when medically necessary.
4. The record must state ongoing functional deficits and why continued skilled therapy is medically necessary.
