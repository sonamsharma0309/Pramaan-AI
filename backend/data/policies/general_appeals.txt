PAYER: ALL
TITLE: General Appeal Submission Standards

A first-level provider appeal must be filed within 180 days of the date of the denial or remittance advice. A complete appeal packet must include the patient identifiers, the claim number, a copy of the denial or remittance advice, the clinical records that support the service, and a statement that references the payer's published coverage criteria.

Peer-to-peer review: the treating physician may request a peer-to-peer conversation with the payer's medical director, which is recommended for medical necessity denials. Expedited review is available when delay would seriously jeopardize the patient's health.

Appeals that cite the specific policy section and map each criterion to a dated chart entry are more likely to be overturned than general statements of medical necessity.
